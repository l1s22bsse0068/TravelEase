Trip Planning Guide

  • Best Time to Visit: May to October for scenic beauty and clear roads.
  • Top Spots: Hunza, Skardu, Naran, Fairy Meadows, and more.
  • Essentials: Hiking shoes, warm layers, travel permits (where needed).
  • Transport: Rent a car or use local jeeps for valleys and mountain roads.
  • Tips: Carry cash, respect local customs, and check weather forecasts.
